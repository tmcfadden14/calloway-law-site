Our Founder

Donald Calloway, Jr.

Principal · Public Interest Counsel

Calloway Law is a boutique public interest practice focused on civil rights, environmental justice, and consumer protection cases — led by Donald Calloway, Jr., counsel whose work sits at the intersection of institutional accountability and consequential litigation.

The firm represents communities and individuals that corporate and government power has failed, and brings to each matter the discipline of an advocate who has argued before courts that matter.

Full Biography →

Calloway Law practices law in the public interest by creating institutional accountability. We represent individuals, businesses, and communities in matters of consequence, bringing disciplined legal judgment, institutional insight, and principled advocacy to every engagement. Our mission is to ensure that power remains accountable, justice remains attainable, and the law serves the people it was created to protect.
